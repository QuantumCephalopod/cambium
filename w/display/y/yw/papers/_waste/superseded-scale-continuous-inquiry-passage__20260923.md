## observed wound

Selecting a Source or Holon from either the Papers overview or a pre-focused root chamber breaks the intended sense of one continuing recursive world.

Observed behavior:
- clustered non-selected contents leave the immediate view;
- the visible chamber/container relation does not appear to recede with those contents;
- root chamber labels remain visually detached in their pre-selection relation;
- the selected organism becomes isolated rather than feeling like one cell reached by scale passage through the still-existing Papers body.

## desired passage

Selection remains a local Papers scale transition:

- the selected Source/Holon moves into the empty center of the Papers r1 Sierpiński body;
- inquiry camera follows/zooms into the selected organism;
- the previously visible chamber structure and all non-selected population remain one coherent background body;
- structure + contents remain in the same Papers root/chamber frame while the camera dives inward; apparent distance/scale comes from perspective through that continuing world, not from pushing the world backward;
- distant metabolights / source embers should read as far-away stars where projection permits;
- the same behavior must hold whether selection begins from overview or from a pre-focused realized chamber.

No new semantic depth, body kind, Display-global navigation law, or container ontology is requested. This is implementation pressure against the already-earned Papers rule: selection is a change of scale inside one continuing world.

## acceptance

1. chamber shells, their labels, and non-selected population share the same inquiry-passage transform;
2. chamber labels no longer use an overview-only fixed camera during selection;
3. pre-focused chamber → organism selection preserves that same chamber/root context as the enclosing world while the camera travels further inward;
4. selected organism remains the same instance/body and reaches inquiry center;
5. previous-scale lights remain perceptible as distant matter rather than being erased by a hard fade;
6. local selection does not mutate global Display navigation/orientation.

Revised acceptance: **camera motion creates depth; background-world translation must remain zero.**
