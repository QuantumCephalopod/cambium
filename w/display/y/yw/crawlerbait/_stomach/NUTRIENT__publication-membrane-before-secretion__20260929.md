## encounter

Crawlerbait already contains a temporal membrane between observation and public embodiment.

The current physiology is not:

`request → immediate public publication`

but:

`HTTP encounter → Tide capture → stored trace → downstream/public secretion`

This creates a previously unarticulated metabolic interval: the encounter exists before it becomes public tissue.

## pressure

The publication boundary may evolve into an immune-like membrane without destroying Crawlerbait's artistic premise.

The purpose is not to sanitize encounters, remove strange matter or define a safe dataset.

The purpose is to distinguish:

`capture = witness`

from

`publication = secretion`

An exact encounter can remain preserved while its crossing into the public membrane is temporarily held.

## possible physiology

`encounter → capture → recognition → release or hold → public secretion`

A held encounter is not deleted, rewritten or silently discarded. It remains unresolved matter awaiting organism-level decision.

A release decision preserves tolerance and prevents the membrane from becoming an overactive filter.

A repeated wound may earn future policy distinction.

A false alarm becomes immune memory for tolerance.

## boundary

This does not authorize a new filter, consent system, moderation layer or separate clean dataset.

It does not decide which encounters should be held.

It only records the earned distinction that a periodic tide creates a meaningful interval between receiving an encounter and publishing a public trace.
