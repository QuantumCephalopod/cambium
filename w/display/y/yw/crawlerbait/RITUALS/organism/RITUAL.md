## static sensor membrane

Crawler requests themselves remain ordinary static CDN/Pages traffic. No bait path or ordinary Crawlerbait read invokes a Worker merely to announce presence.

Cloudflare is a passive observation provider. A periodic tide may query a bounded historical analytics window after requests have already happened. Provider telemetry is evidence of HTTP contact, not authenticated actor identity or intent.

Raw provider telemetry is never copied wholesale into the organism. Raw IP addresses are not admitted. Raw User-Agent strings are not persisted as public identity; only bounded family tokens and short one-way signatures may survive the local boundary.

## bounded tide

One tide performs one bounded pass:

`Cloudflare observation → z Boundary → x Continuity → y Adaptation → w Sediment`

The current policy caps path shape, depth, candidate count, total grown routes, signatures per route, and new routes per tide. Traffic may supply pressure but cannot modify those limits.

Only safe repeated 404 pressure can create a new route in v1. A route that crosses the gate is rendered as later static HTML/JSON under `w/public/`. No-change tides leave organism bytes unchanged.

## public route law

Crawlerbait-local HTTP routes such as `/login` or `/admin` are not global Display addresses and are not CCCC vertices. They are outward apertures generated by the `w · Sediment` child from admitted local state.

The public reef is deliberately crawlable and ordinary. Human-facing Display may visualize the same state as tracks, crabpots, fossils, moiré or Sierpiński-like fields, but that phenomenology remains identity-owned and must not leak into central Display semantics.
