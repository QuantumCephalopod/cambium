## encounter

Crawlerbait already has real traffic Beings with kind-specific bodies and a crawlercam, while the older body nutrient still leaves "meeting one being by pointer" open.

The new encounter gives that open relation a concrete instrument:

> the shared Display droplet can lock onto a Crawlerbait Being.

The droplet becomes a local meeting lens rather than requiring another panel.

## Being encounter

When the lens softly locks to one Being, it should reveal the public identity of **that artwork Being** and the body facts Crawlerbait is already allowed to publish.

The word "identity" here is bounded by the current membrane:

- public `beingId` / artwork-local identity may be shown;
- kind may be shown;
- public encounter/growth/body facts may be shown;
- irreversible public phenotype/body may be shown.

The lens must **not** reveal or reconstruct:

- literal IP;
- internal network pseudonym;
- exact User-Agent;
- readable auxiliary provider dimensions;
- private recognition inputs.

The Being is the public organism. Its private recognition substrate is not visitor content.

## visit-grown morphology

The body should visibly carry its history of repeated encounters.

Philipp's pressure is procedural growth of body parts based on visits so recurrence becomes shape rather than only a counter.

This should extend, not replace, the existing kind grammar:

- kind still supplies the broad body law;
- the Being's own encounters/visits grow or differentiate parts within that law;
- two Beings of the same kind can therefore become visibly different through their actual histories;
- growth is replayable from public Crawlerbait encounter tissue.

No visit may create a body feature that cannot be regenerated from public-safe Crawlerbait state.

## temporal growth

Where possible, the body should be able to replay its growth through time:

- before an encounter-earned part exists, it is absent;
- when the relevant visit occurs, that part can emerge;
- current body is the accumulated truthful result.

This connects the existing historical growth-replay pressure to the individual Being body rather than adding a decorative animation.

## LOD / lock relation

Ordinary Crawlerbait coarse-resolution law remains.

A lens lock may temporarily ask the locked Being for a finer representation while the rest of the reef stays coarse.

That finer representation is still the same Being and must not bypass current coalescence/identity rules outside the lens.

## acceptance

1. the shared Display lens can lock to a real Crawlerbait Being through a generic coupling;
2. the locked lens identifies the public artwork Being without disclosing recognition substrate;
3. kind and encounter-earned morphology remain distinguishable;
4. body growth is deterministic/replayable from public-safe encounter state;
5. repeated visits can produce visible body differentiation inside the existing kind grammar;
6. temporal replay never shows a body part before the encounter that earns it;
7. coarse reef LOD remains intact outside the lens;
8. no new overlay/panel is required to "meet" the Being;
9. the same lens mechanism remains usable by non-Crawlerbait sites.

This nutrient does not reopen Crawlerbait's privacy membrane.
