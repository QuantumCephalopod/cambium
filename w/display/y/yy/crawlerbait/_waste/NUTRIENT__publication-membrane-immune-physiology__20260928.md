## encounter

Crawlerbait's artistic drive depends on preserving the literal encounter with the open web rather than pre-sanitizing the world into a safe or curated dataset. Paths, query strings, probes, hostile/weird payloads and other attacker-controlled request matter are interesting precisely because they witness what machines and people actually do when an open surface exists.

Crawlerbait already has a periodic membrane rather than a live public-write path. The user identified that this temporal gap can become a literal immune function:

`observe exactly → hold before public secretion when warranted → quarantine without destruction → decide separately → release or let the real case grow policy`

Quarantine is not deletion, redaction, HMAC-by-default, semantic moderation or a second cleaned dataset. The exact encounter remains preserved. The unresolved distinction is only whether an encounter has crossed the public membrane yet.

Policy should grow from encountered cases, not hypothetical edge cases:
- a held encounter may later be released verbatim, teaching tolerance;
- a real wound may earn a new response/policy distinction;
- recurrence may grow immune memory;
- pre-emptive overfiltering that destroys the artistic/observational substrate is an autoimmunity failure.

The "immune system" is currently a relational description of Crawlerbait's existing physiology, not authority to create a new top-level immune-system organ/folder. Let current shared-root + Crawlerbait-local RITUALS determine whether the pressure belongs within existing `z · Membrane`, its relation with `y · Tide`, or later earns differentiation.
