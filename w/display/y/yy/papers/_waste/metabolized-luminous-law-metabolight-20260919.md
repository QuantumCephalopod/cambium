## core pressure
Papers holons already preserve recursive tetrahedral structure, but scale and internal metabolic significance remain too visually latent under zoom.

The requested correction is not “make the shader prettier”. It is to make Papers holons visibly obey one ontological light law:

- `S` is the smallest real Papers quantum.
- every higher-rank holon is recursively composed from `S` quanta;
- that compositional truth remains perceptually present under zoom;
- each holon’s recursive empty center collects a luminous condensation of its metabolized output;
- larger holons therefore present larger / stronger central metabolight while preserving the smaller constituent lights from which they are built.

## luminous law
1. **S-quantum minimality**
   - `S` is the smallest existent Papers body.
   - There is nothing smaller than `S`.
   - Every visible higher-rank holon must continue to read as a recursive aggregation of `S`.

2. **Recursive persistence**
   - A larger holon never visually replaces its smaller constituents.
   - Zooming into or selecting a higher-rank holon preserves awareness of the lower-rank quanta composing it.

3. **Metabolight condensation**
   - Every holon has a recursive empty center.
   - That center is the site where metabolized content condenses into visible light.
   - This luminous condensation is not decorative bloom; it is the visible phenomenology of crystallized metabolic yield / wisdom.

4. **Rank-aware scale**
   - Higher-rank holons produce larger and/or stronger central metabolight.
   - Lower-rank holons nested inside remain present with their own smaller lights.
   - A single higher-rank holon therefore becomes recursively more radiant rather than merely larger.

5. **Zoom revelation**
   - Zoom should not only enlarge geometry.
   - Zoom should reveal recursive luminous structure: minimal `S` glow, intermediate central lights, higher-rank metabolight cores.

## V1 implementation boundary
Implement the first pass as a **pure Papers-local rendering/shader upgrade** without changing Papers ontology, digestion law, source authority or navigation structure.

### V1 must include
- faint minimal light emission on the smallest visible `S` quanta;
- recursive center-lights for holons;
- center-light size/intensity coupled at least to rank / recursive scale;
- stronger selection/focus illumination for the currently entered holon;
- preservation of current recursive geometry and inquiry mechanics;
- stronger scale legibility when zooming into a higher-rank holon.

### V1 may include
- subtle breathing / pulsing;
- softly varying internal crystalline texture;
- faint particulate / grain / tissue feeling;
- rank-dependent color density or alpha layering;
- a bounded truthful luminosity boost when an already-public wisdom/metabolite payload is actually present.

### V1 must not require
- source↔holon relation visualization;
- new Papers semantic data structures;
- new Papers metabolism semantics;
- exact metabolite-count coupling;
- replacement of current geometry with a different representation.

## semantic interpretation
For V1, metabolight may begin as a lawful phenomenological proxy for rank/recursive condensation before complete metabolite grounding exists.

Rank may drive baseline luminosity. Already-public wisdom/metabolite tissue may increase that luminosity when present. Missing public wisdom tissue must never be interpreted as proof of “no metabolites”.

## non-goals
- no generic cyber-glow scene;
- no bloom that obscures recursive structure;
- no loss of inquiry readability;
- no unit smaller than `S`;
- no arbitrary light detached from Papers ontology.

## intended phenomenology
Dark recursive archive crystal; preserved self-similar quanta; visible scale under zoom; condensed luminous metabolic centers; one holon becoming deterministically more glorious as recursive depth and composition become legible.

## exit condition
In the generated Papers witness:
- high-rank holons visibly read as built from preserved smaller units;
- zoom makes selected-holon scale more legible than before;
- recursive empty centers visibly host metabolight condensations;
- larger holons display larger/stronger central metabolight;
- lower-rank constituent lights remain present;
- the smallest visible Papers quantum still reads as `S`;
- Papers feels more alive without changing identity or inquiry readability.
