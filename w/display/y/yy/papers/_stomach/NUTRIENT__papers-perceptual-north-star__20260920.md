## live pump reacquisition — four current Papers pumps

The current live spawn-source surface confirms exactly four enabled Papers pumps:

1. `PUMP.papers - accumulation` → `accumulation-heartbeat`
2. `PUMP.papers - chaperone` → `chaperoning-heartbeat`
3. `PUMP.papers - digestion` → `digestion-heartbeat`
4. `PUMP.papers - anabolism` → `anabolism-heartbeat`

These are temporal wake channels into current organism-local receptors; pump titles are not the semantic law themselves.

### 1 · accumulation — curiosity creates appetite

The current Papers body is reacquired and three Mnemos perspectives run a genuine trialogue until a sharp unresolved pressure/question emerges.

Only then is an acquisition contract formed and external candidates are sought. At most one candidate crosses the nutritional threshold in one heartbeat.

A successful accumulation heartbeat does **not** digest or activate the source. It admits one unresolved acquisition nutrient / procurement demand into the stomach.

Public-facing invariant:
**the current body notices what it cannot yet answer and develops an appetite for one external encounter that could change it.**

### membrane between 1 and 2 · procurement is real but is not one of the four live Papers pumps

The intake receptor separately defines `procurement-heartbeat`: an admitted demand may be lawfully embodied as a complete verified non-derived local carrier.

This is not currently a fifth `PUMP.papers` source.

Therefore a four-phase public explanation should not falsely rename Chaperone as procurement. Procurement is better represented as the material crossing of the external membrane between appetite and preparation.

### 2 · chaperone — arrived matter is made metabolically legible

Chaperone inspects unresolved arrived matter and the downstream enzymes that could accept it.

It distinguishes carrier, receipt, compound lead, auxiliary tissue, routing fiber and indigesta; verifies source identity/authorship/provenance where required; binds related matter; and folds/routes one target toward a truthful downstream state such as READY_FOR_SOURCE_METABOLISM, PROCUREMENT_REQUIRED, AUXILIARY or INDIGESTA.

It does not digest the source or grow the semantic body.

Public-facing invariant:
**matter that has entered Papers is recognized, checked and folded into a form the organism can actually digest.**

### 3 · digestion — one complete external source becomes one Source organism

Digestion selects one eligible complete source carrier/bundle and saturates the chosen carrier rather than reading only a summary.

The source is divided into exactly four faithful source bodies whose union exhausts the carrier. Their complete relational closure is then derived:
- 4 source bodies;
- 6 pair relations;
- 4 triple relations;
- 1 whole-volume closure;
- collapsed surviving metabolites.

If closure is earned, one canonical `S.*` Source organism is individuated/persisted. Its four internal vertices are source-body partitions, **not** four Papers parent organisms.

Public-facing invariant:
**one outside work is metabolized into the minimum living Papers quantum: one tetrahedral Source organism.**

### 4 · anabolism — four living organisms become one higher-rank whole

Anabolism reads the live FIELD and selects exactly four distinct ACTIVE same-rank Papers organisms:
- four `S` may birth one `1H`;
- four `(n-1)H` may birth one `nH`;
- mixed ranks are forbidden.

The four selected organisms are handed to the same relational-body engine for a genuine 4V/6E/4F/1T closure.

Only if the complete relation closes does one fresh next-rank Holon enter FIELD + LINEAGE.

Parents are **not consumed**. They remain ACTIVE and may participate in future births.

The newborn may itself later become one of four same-rank parents for another anabolic birth.

Public-facing invariant:
**four already-living bodies can cohere into one new larger body without ceasing to exist themselves; recursive growth can therefore continue without predefined depth.**

## first perceptual collapse

The four live pumps genuinely support a public four-phase cycle:

**APPETITE → PREPARE → METABOLIZE → GROW**

or, in more organismic language:

**QUESTION → FOLD → DIGEST → COALESCE**

The phases are cyclical rather than a one-way pipeline:
Anabolism changes the living body; the changed body is exactly what the next Accumulation heartbeat reacquires, so new growth changes what Papers can notice and what it becomes curious about next.

Do not map these four temporal phases onto Papers' four root loci merely because both sets have cardinality four. Root loci are spatial/semantic anatomy; pumps are recurrent temporal physiology.
