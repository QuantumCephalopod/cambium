## encounter

The current production Papers view now exposes more recursive scale hierarchy, but the user explicitly wants to stop optimizing implementation and determine what *feeling* and perceptual meaning Papers should produce first.

The current screenshot reads as several bright recursive clusters suspended in a large dark field. This is technically more hierarchical than the earlier fixed-proxy body, but it does not yet clearly communicate what Papers itself is as a whole.

## pressure

Resolve the perceptual ontology before further rendering work.

Questions to metabolize:
- What should a person feel they are looking at when they enter Papers?
- What is the whole-body percept before any organism is selected?
- What should Sources feel like relative to Holons?
- What should increasing rank mean perceptually besides simply becoming larger/brighter?
- What should empty space, the four root loci, recursion, lineage and metabolight each contribute to the experience?
- Should Papers read primarily as archive, cosmos, tissue, organism, ecology, crystal, map, or another form — and which of those metaphors survive contact with the actual organism law?
- How should zoom/selection feel: clicking an object, entering tissue, changing scale, following ancestry, or some combination?
- What must remain immediately legible without text?

## boundary

No implementation change is authorized by this nutrient yet.

The next act should derive a coherent perceptual north star and a small set of invariant experiential relations from the already-earned Papers ontology before translating them back into renderer behavior.
