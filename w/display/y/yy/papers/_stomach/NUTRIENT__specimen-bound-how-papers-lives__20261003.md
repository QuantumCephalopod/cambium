## encounter

The current explanatory animation has served as a prototype, but it is still an explanatory object beside the real organism.

The desired next form is:

> explain Papers **through one actual living specimen**, not through dummy/placeholder geometry or a separate explanatory overlay.

The explanation should become a visitor-like traversal of a real rendered Holon.

## dynamic specimen

Choose a real, currently existing **rank-4 Holon** from the public Papers population.

The specimen is not canonically special. It is chosen dynamically from the eligible rank-4 population precisely because any rank-4 Holon should be explainable by the same method.

That is part of the proof:

- the selected identity may change;
- its content may change;
- the explanatory procedure must still work;
- the explanation demonstrates self-similarity because it depends on invariant formation law, not on one hand-authored example.

No dummy or placeholder Holon is allowed as a silent fallback.

If no truthful rank-4 specimen is available in the current projection, the explanation must expose/withhold that state rather than fabricate one.

## specimen-bound dramaturgy

The real specimen itself is the explanatory surface.

The sequence should use the same body and same structural relations a visitor can encounter manually:

- locate / encounter the specimen;
- reveal or descend through its earned structure;
- expose parent/child/closure relations at the moment they become relevant;
- show the same rank/body grammar recurring;
- return without mutating the specimen's semantic truth.

Whether the first implementation narrates bottom-up or top-down is secondary. It must not invent a second traversal grammar.

## Pretext commentary

Explanation appears as **timed Pretext commentary attached to the actual encounter**.

This is not a conventional tooltip system.

Commentary should arrive artistically at the moment the relevant structure is visible, then yield back to the specimen.

The comments describe what is actually being shown; they must not get ahead of the current body state.

Pretext remains layout/ink. Papers remains semantic owner of the explanation.

## replacement pressure

The external/prototype explanatory animation should not survive merely because it already exists.

Once the specimen-bound explanation can carry the same explanatory function truthfully, the old demonstration should be retired or reduced rather than duplicated.

The overview's central field should remain a living field, not become a second tutorial canvas.

2. the same explanation logic works across multiple eligible rank-4 Holons;
3. no dummy/placeholder body is used;

The randomness is allowed only over eligible real specimens; self-similarity, not specimen identity, is the invariant being taught.
