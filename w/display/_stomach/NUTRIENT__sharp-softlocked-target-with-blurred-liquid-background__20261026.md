## Admitted target invariant

While the shared drop has a valid softlock, the actual target stays sharp and its background becomes strongly defocused inside the actual drop footprint. Selected text must become visibly readable. Both field and body/text contacts participate. Preserve the drop's identity, moving target, source wording and continuation; missing projection is not source withdrawal.

## Required separation

Foreground target pixels/coverage and background scene must be distinguishable before focus composition. Blurring the already-flattened target/background cannot satisfy this. An unblurred rectangular copy of the whole reading area also fails: it keeps unrelated background sharp and recreates the cut. Alpha/coverage, an admitted target draw, or an owner-lent foreground must identify the actual target; Display cannot guess identity/meaning from colour, text strings or source schema.

Background retains continuous liquid refraction. Focus blur stays inside the actual drop silhouette with no rectangular halo or exterior spill. Target sharpness and light text shadow survive final composition. The existing shared lock/departure channel remains the only interaction instrument.
