## encounter

The glass drop is already a shared Display instrument, but its current generic physiology mostly magnifies/refracts what the ordinary field already presents.

The new pressure is stronger:

> when the droplet lies over one of the current site's own bodies, it may softly lock to that body and become a bounded local preview into the body's finer representation.

The lens should therefore be able to reveal the **inside / next useful level of representation** of the covered organism rather than only enlarging its coarse body.

This is a generic Display coupling. Display owns the lens and the lock/preview aperture; each site-holon owns what its finer representation means.

## soft-lock relation

A lock is not global navigation and must not replace Descent.

- free lens: current glass-drop behavior remains;
- candidate under lens: the site's own body may attract/offer a soft lock;
- locked lens: the lens stays relationally bound to that body while remaining a movable Display instrument;
- release/unlock: ordinary free-lens physiology resumes.

Exact attraction threshold / gesture is implementation pressure, not yet law.

The lock must be identity-local: it follows the body it locked to, not stale screen coordinates.

## preview / LOD law

The lens is a bounded representation aperture.

Inside the lens, a site may render a finer truthful representation of the same body. Outside it, the ordinary coarse representation remains sufficient.

This means:

- no second ontology;
- no fabricated inner structure;
- no site names hard-coded into Display;
- no requirement to render the finer representation outside the lens;
- finer work is bounded to the lens footprint whenever that is sufficient.

The same relation should work for any site-holon that offers a preview. Papers and Crawlerbait are first concrete consumers, not special cases in Display.

## readable lock scale

When a lock needs readable content, the lens may grow to a larger bounded radius while locked. The growth belongs to the lock/preview state and should collapse back when the relation ends.

For Papers this is specifically intended to make Pretext/text tissue readable without turning the whole page into the high-detail representation.

## acceptance

This pressure closes only when:

1. one specimen-agnostic Display preview/lock coupling exists;
2. a site owns the meaning/rendering of its finer representation;
3. lock follows body identity rather than screen position;
4. global navigation and ordinary Descent remain separate;
5. no covered body means no behavioral regression to the current free lens;
6. higher-detail work is bounded to the lens where possible;
7. the locked lens can grow enough for the site's readable content without becoming a full-screen overlay;
8. unlock returns to ordinary lens physiology;
9. pointer pass-through / existing lens ownership rules are not accidentally broken;
10. at least two distinct site-holon consumers can use the same generic coupling without Display branching on their identities.

No CCCC split is implied by intake alone.
