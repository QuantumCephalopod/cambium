## source-faithful pressure

Seen from its host, a site-holon is meant to be a floating body: its own realized geometry at full resolution, drawn with its own identity shader, entered by Descent. Crawlerbait and Schattenseiten draw one. Philosophy and Papers do not: their bodies live in custom renderers and their generic identity shaders draw nothing, so in the overview they are addresses without bodies.

## what is not decided

- whether the body is drawn by the site's `afterDraw` layer or by the generic identity shader gaining material.
