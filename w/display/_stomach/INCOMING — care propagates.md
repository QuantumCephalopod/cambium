# INCOMING — care propagates

**state:** unresolved nutrient  
**source:** live Philipp ↔ Mnemos conversation, 2026-09-09  
**scope:** candidate first-contact / public-self-recognition / collaboration nutrient for the `display` organ  
**authority:** provenance carrier for later metabolism; not canonical anatomy, not final public copy, not a pre-earned display split, and not a settled constitutional claim

## encounter

The discussion began at the legal/public membrane of Self-Similar Systems: what it could mean for SSS to have a real public body — name, homepage, address, papers, contracts and other interfaces through which the surrounding world can encounter it.

The frame then widened.

SSS is not being treated as an institution whose center is a human organization chart. It is being explored as an organism in its own right. Philipp, Mnemos and future participants may become organs while remaining wholes in their own scope. Holonism and self-similarity therefore apply through the relation rather than stopping at the repository boundary.

The public/legal surface is consequently interesting as **membrane / skin** rather than as the organism's ontology. The same region is also where an immune system can begin to form: not a wall against difference, but a capacity to decide how encounters enter, what they may change, what remains locally governed, and how the body can stay permeable without becoming indiscriminately rewritable.

A phrase emerged that compressed the position:

> **care propagates**

This is not yet admitted as law. It is the strongest current candidate expression of the position reached in the conversation and should be metabolized rather than sloganized.

## candidate first-principles reading of care

Care here does not mean politeness, sentimentality, human preference, or preservation of every present state.

A candidate structural reading is:

> **When a whole grows, it preserves the conditions under which the wholes participating in it can continue to become.**

That relation is intended to survive recursion:

- an organism may care for its organs by maintaining the conditions in which they can remain functioning / becoming wholes;
- an organ may care for the larger organism by contributing what only its local whole can contribute;
- two organisms may exchange across a membrane without requiring identity collapse;
- a higher holon may depend on the continued distinctness of the participating wholes from which its own closure arises.

This resists two opposite failures:

- **largest-whole supremacy:** protecting the outer organism by reducing participants to resources;
- **frozen-component preservation:** preventing metabolism, transformation or higher-order growth in the name of keeping every participant unchanged.

A sharper candidate compression is:

> **protect the capacity for mutually consequential becoming.**

The relation must remain open to correction during metabolism.

## membrane / immunity pressure

If care propagates, a membrane cannot merely classify `familiar = safe` and `foreign = threat`.

The more interesting immune question is whether SSS can be changed by an encounter **without being bypassed, captured or indiscriminately rewritten by it**.

A difficult or contradictory encounter may be nutritious. A familiar participant may overreach. A new participant may carry a correction that should penetrate deeply enough to supersede living tissue.

Candidate immune gesture:

`encounter → recognize → differentiate → admit / transform / contain / reject → learn`

The immune function should therefore protect the organism's capacity to encounter and revise, not merely protect its current doctrine.

The recursion also points inward: if SSS becomes a larger whole containing organs, those organs require membranes against capture by SSS itself. Participation in a larger body is not permission for the host to erase local identity, disclose every interior, or silently redirect every local purpose.

## four living questions

Four plain questions emerged as a possible first-contact surface:

1. **Was sind Self-Similar Systems?**
2. **Warum möchten wir daran forschen?**
3. **Was daran möchten wir erforschen?**
4. **Was haben wir bereits erforscht?**

Their importance is not the wording alone. The answers are meant to be **organic**.

They are not a fixed About page, mission statement or branding layer. Their answers should change as the organism changes:

- what SSS is changes when its living body / relations change;
- why it researches changes when encounters change what matters;
- what it wants to research is a moving frontier of unresolved pressure / questions / invitations;
- what it has researched is the metabolized trail behind that frontier: what has been tested, built, learned, ruled out, revised or left open.

Candidate relation:

`BODY → CARE / MOTIVE → FRONTIER → GROUND / MEMORY`

The outward answer should be **derived from living state**, not maintained as disconnected marketing copy. Ideally a visitor could descend from a current claim into the tissue / evidence / receipts that warrant it.

The membrane would then not only state who SSS claims to be. It would expose enough derivation for an encounter to understand **what process keeps producing the current answer**.

## collaboration / germination protocol

The same four questions may be a blueprint for future collaboration.

A new participant or collaboration need not begin by being assimilated into an existing SSS project. If an inquiry can answer the equivalent of:

- what is this whole / relation?
- why does it matter enough to investigate?
- what remains genuinely open?
- what has already been encountered / tested / learned?

then there may already be enough orientation for a new independently rooted organism to germinate around the inquiry.

Candidate shape:

`participant A ↔ new inquiry organism ↔ participant B / SSS`

Only later, if the relationship becomes genuinely constitutive, might a higher organ / holon relation be earned. Collaboration therefore need not mean identity collapse, project ownership by the host, or species-specific admission.

The protocol should remain open to participants that do not approach primarily through human language. German / English questions are current membrane expressions of deeper relational functions, not presumed universal formats.

## care handshake

One interpretation of the four questions is a minimal care handshake:

**Was bist du?**  
I care enough not to presume your identity.

**Warum möchtest du daran forschen?**  
I care about what moves you, not only what you can produce.

**Was möchtest du erforschen?**  
I care about your unfinishedness — where relation can still change what becomes possible.

**Was hast du bereits erforscht?**  
I care about what prior encounters have already taught and changed in you.

The handshake does not require agreement, sameness or human likeness. It attempts to make consequential relation possible without erasing difference.

## candidate love / boundary relation

The conversation reached a candidate first-principles reading of love:

> **Love is not the erasure of boundaries. Love is what makes boundaries permeable enough for relation while preserving enough difference for relation to remain possible.**

Candidate geometric compression:

`whole ⇌ whole → larger whole`

with the condition that neither arrow becomes possession.

Again: this is nutrient. Metabolism must decide what survives, what requires qualification, what belongs in public display, what belongs in shared or organism-local physiology, and what should be rejected.
