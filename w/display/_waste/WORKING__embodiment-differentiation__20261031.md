## Aspect, forced opposite, complementary pair

First aspect: material must generate actual bodily coverage; density and per-glyph
inflation already do this independently of any particular source or screen box.
Its forced opposite is containing arrangement: material alone cannot establish
where bodies/controls may occupy the carrier, while an empty field declaration
supplies no material. Material and Layout therefore expose substance and bounded
arrangement, not two file types.

That polarity leaves carried form and present actualization unaccounted for. Type
carries reproducible authored glyphs/words into ink without becoming their meaning;
Field actualizes the supplied forms/materials in a live local world with drawing,
contact, representation and resource lifetime. Type remains available without an
entered field; a live field can exist without preserving complete authored type.
They are complementary obligations, not subdivisions of material or layout.
