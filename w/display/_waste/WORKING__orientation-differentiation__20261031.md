## Derivation: aspect, opposite, complementary pair

An undeniable aspect is the Frame: an encounter needs a realized spatial field in
which a place can exist. Its forced complement is Passage: constructing a possible
place cannot itself make an accepted encounter become the current place; a crossing
must be actualized and return/cancellation remain possible. These poles distinguish
possible spatial relation from its realized encounter movement.

That pair does not preserve how one continuing viewer is oriented, nor govern what
may be exposed/contacted now. Bearing carries the same oriented relation through
rotation, inspection and remounting. Focus bounds exposed/occupied/contactable
space and optical attention. This complementary pair is independent of constructing
geometry and completing a crossing. A static field can carry bearing and focus
without crossing; a successful fold alone cannot supply either.
