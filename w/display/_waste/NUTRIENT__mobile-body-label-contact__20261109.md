# Encounter: overlapping body labels on the mobile field

Philipp asked to care for the mobile view. On the 320 × 568 and 390 × 844 Chromium Canvas2D overview, widening Philosophy's addressed viewport makes the actual bodies larger but Schattenseiten and Crawlerbait still have overlapping native title buttons. A touch visitor can see competing names at the same location. The actual body, its identity, native button and geometric anchor must remain truthful and selectable; do not move or repack the world bodies, change their semantic paths, or pretend a different renderer or physical device has been witnessed. Examine whether shared, anchor-connected label layout can preserve readable contact in the available screen space.
